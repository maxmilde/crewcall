A {{worker}} was offered one or more shifts and replied in free text. Work out what they said.

Return this JSON object:
{
  "label": "yes" | "no" | "partial" | "question" | "unclear",
  "accepted_shift_ids": ["shift id", ...],
  "question": "the {{worker}}'s question" or null,
  "constraints": ["standing constraint", ...],
  "too_far": true or false
}

Rules:
- yes: accepts every offered shift. no: accepts none. partial: accepts some shifts but not all; list exactly those ids.
- question: they ask something (e.g. about the place or the hours) instead of deciding; put the question in "question". If they accept some shifts and also ask something, use partial or yes and still fill "question".
- A yes with a condition attached (must leave before the end, can only come later, wants to bring someone, needs something first) is NOT an acceptance of that shift. "X works if ...", "yes as long as ..." and "yes but only if ..." are conditional: do not list it, and put the condition in "question" so the owner decides. Use question when no shift is cleanly accepted.
- "wrong number", "stop messaging me", "not interested" are no.
- unclear: anything ambiguous. Do not guess.
- Match days by name or date: "only Thursday" means the shift on Thursday. Only use ids from the offered list.
- constraints: lasting rules about their availability stated in the reply, written short in English, e.g. "never weekends", "nothing after 18:00", "too far from Žižkov". Only rules they state as general ("never", "always", "I don't do..."). One-off answers to this offer ("only Thursday", "busy this Friday", "I have to leave at 6 that day") are NOT constraints. Empty if none.
- too_far: true when the distance or travel time is a reason they decline.
